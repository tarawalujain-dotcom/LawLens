🔒 Privacy

Privacy involves how personal information is collected, used, stored, and shared.

Before sharing personal information, consider why it is needed and who may have access to it.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Think about what personal information is being collected.
  2. Ask why the information is needed and how it will be used.
  3. Avoid sharing sensitive information when it is not necessary.
  4. If you are concerned, talk to a trusted adult.

Privacy rights and rules can differ depending on the situation and local laws.

Source: UNICEF — Convention on the Rights of the Child

🛡️ Bullying & Harassment

Bullying can involve repeated harmful behavior, including physical, verbal, social, or online behavior.

You do not have to deal with a serious or unsafe situation alone.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Keep a record of what happened.
  2. Tell a trusted adult, teacher, or school counselor.
  3. If the situation continues, use the appropriate school reporting process.
  4. If you feel unsafe, seek help from a trusted adult or relevant authority.

The right steps can depend on the situation and local rules.

Source: UNESCO — Safe Learning Environments

⚖️ Fair Treatment

Students may have concerns when they believe they are being treated differently or unfairly in an educational setting.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Write down what happened and when it happened.
  2. Ask for an explanation if you feel you were treated unfairly.
  3. Talk to a trusted teacher, adult, or school counselor.
  4. If needed, use the appropriate complaint process.

What counts as unfair treatment can vary by situation and local rules.

Source: UNESCO — Safe Learning Environments

🗣️ Speaking Up & Getting Help

If something concerns you at school, you can start by explaining what happened to someone you trust.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Write down what happened and why you are concerned.
  2. Talk to a trusted teacher, adult, or school counselor.
  3. Ask what support or options are available to you.
  4. If the problem continues, follow the appropriate reporting process.

Available options can depend on the situation and school policies.

🔐 Online Privacy

Online privacy includes the information you share with websites, apps, platforms, and other people online.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Check what personal information an app or website asks for.
  2. Review your privacy settings regularly.
  3. Think carefully before sharing personal information online.
  4. If something feels unsafe, talk to a trusted adult.

Privacy protections can vary by platform, country, and situation.

Source: UNICEF — Online Privacy

🛡️ Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying refers to harmful or bullying behavior that happens through digital platforms or communication tools.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Save screenshots or other evidence.
  2. Block or report the account when appropriate.
  3. Tell a trusted adult, teacher, or school counselor.
  4. If you feel unsafe, seek help from a trusted authority.

The best next step can depend on the situation and local rules.

Source: UNESCO — Safe Learning Environments

📸 Sharing Photos

Sharing photos online can involve privacy, consent, and control over personal images.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Think before sharing a photo of yourself or someone else.
  2. Ask for permission before sharing someone else's photo.
  3. Check who can see the photo and where it may be shared.
  4. If a photo is shared without permission, tell a trusted adult and report it when appropriate.

Rules about images, privacy, and consent vary by country and situation.

🔐 Staying Safe Online

Online safety involves protecting your accounts, information, and personal boundaries.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Use strong passwords and keep them private.
  2. Check privacy and security settings.
  3. Be careful with links and unknown people online.
  4. If something makes you feel unsafe, talk to a trusted adult.

🛒 Buying & Returning

When buying something, keep information about the purchase and understand the seller's policies.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Keep your receipt or proof of purchase.
  2. Check the seller's return or refund policy.
  3. Explain the problem clearly and ask what options are available.
  4. If you cannot resolve the issue, keep records and seek appropriate help.

Return, refund, and consumer rights vary by country and situation.

Source: UNCTAD — Consumer Protection

📝 Agreements

Agreements can create responsibilities for the people involved. It is important to understand what you are accepting.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Read the agreement carefully.
  2. Make sure you understand what you are agreeing to.
  3. Ask questions if something is unclear.
  4. Keep a copy of important agreements or documents.

The legal effect of an agreement depends on the situation and local laws.

⚠️ Something Went Wrong

If something goes wrong with a purchase or service, keeping clear records can help you explain what happened.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Write down what happened and keep relevant evidence.
  2. Contact the person or business involved.
  3. Keep records of messages, receipts, or documents.
  4. If unresolved, look for the appropriate complaint or support process.
Source: UNCTAD — Consumer Protection

⚖️ Legal Help

LawLens provides general educational information. For a specific legal problem, you may need help from a qualified professional or an appropriate support service.

🧭 What Can You Do?

  1. Write down your situation and your main questions.
  2. Collect relevant documents or evidence.
  3. Talk to a trusted adult if you are unsure what to do.
  4. Look for a qualified legal professional or support service when needed.
